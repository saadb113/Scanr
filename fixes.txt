   REFUND POLICY — SCANR

   Last updated: March 2026

   Our Stance on Refunds
   Scanr is a digital software product. Due to the nature of 
   digital goods, all sales are final once a license key has 
   been issued and activated.

   1. No Refund Policy
   We do not offer refunds once:
   - A license key has been generated and sent to you
   - The license key has been activated on an account
   - You have accessed the platform and its features

   2. Before You Buy
   We strongly encourage you to:
   - Read the full feature list on our features page
   - Watch the demo video before purchasing
   - Contact us on WhatsApp with any questions before payment
   - Test the platform on demo mode before connecting real funds
   We are happy to answer any questions before you commit to a purchase.

   3. Exceptions
   We will consider a refund ONLY in these situations:
   - You were charged twice for the same purchase (duplicate payment)
   - Your license key was never delivered and we cannot resolve 
     the issue within 48 hours
   - A technical issue on our end prevents you from accessing 
     the platform and we cannot fix it within 72 hours
   In these cases contact us on WhatsApp immediately with 
   proof of payment.

   4. Subscription Cancellations
   If you are on a monthly or yearly subscription:
   - You can cancel anytime from your account settings
   - Cancellation stops future charges immediately
   - No refund is issued for the current billing period
   - You retain access until the end of your paid period

   5. Demo Mode
   Scanr offers a full demo mode that lets you test all features 
   including the AI Agent and Strategy Bot without real funds. 
   We strongly recommend using demo mode before going live.
   The availability of demo mode means feature-related refund 
   requests will not be considered.

   6. Chargebacks
   Filing a chargeback without contacting us first will result 
   in permanent suspension of your license with no possibility 
   of reinstatement. Please contact us first — we are always 
   willing to resolve issues fairly.

   7. Contact Us
   For any payment issues or concerns contact us via:
   - WhatsApp: (fetched from admin settings)
   - Contact form: scanr.online
   We aim to respond within 24 hours.

💰 Position Sizing — How It Works
📊 Position Size Formula
Risk Amount = Capital × Risk%
SL Distance = |Entry − Stop Loss|
Qty = Risk Amount ÷ SL Distance
Margin = (Qty × Entry) ÷ Max Leverage
Fees = Qty × Entry × Fee% × 2
⚡ Leverage = Bybit Max
After AI analysis, position size is calculated using Bybit's maximum leverage for that coin. Clicking "Trade on Bybit" pre-fills the terminal with that leverage.
✅ When It Shows
After analyzing a coin with AI, a Position Size card appears — configure capital & risk in the Bybit tab.
⚠️ Risk-based position sizer — not financial advice.

Privacy notice.

A short explanation of how the Scanr launch site handles email signups.

Launch updates

If you submit your email and check the consent box, Scanr uses your address to send occasional product progress and launch updates. This site does not sell or rent subscriber email addresses.

Where signup data goes

Signups are saved in a private local JSON file at data/subscribers.json (or the folder configured with SCANR_DATA_DIR) and appear in the password-protected /admin dashboard. If SUBSCRIBE_WEBHOOK_URL is configured, signups are also forwarded to that mailing-list provider. Visitors can opt in to anonymous page-view counting and approximate country totals. Scanr does not store full visitor IP addresses. If the hosting platform does not provide a country code, the server sends the visitor IP to ipapi.co to estimate the country; Scanr does not retain that IP.

If the launch-list provider is not configured on a deployment with REQUIRE_SUBSCRIBE_WEBHOOK=true, the site will not accept submissions and will show a clear message instead of pretending the email was saved.

Retention and removal

Your address should be retained only while it is needed to provide Scanr launch updates. Use the unsubscribe option provided in each launch email, or contact support@scanr.me to request removal.

Changes

This notice is for the coming-soon prototype. It should be reviewed and updated before collecting signups at scale or launching a broader product privacy policy.

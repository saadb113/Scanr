THE SCANR IDEA

Less tab switching.
More market context.

Crypto tools can feel scattered. Scanr is being shaped around a simple flow: find what matters, understand the setup, and review decisions with context.

⌕01 / DISCOVER

More than 100 charts, one view

Compare more than 100 charts on one screen. Scanr aims to check 100–200+ coins in minutes and show you which ones may be worth a closer look.

Explore scanning ↗
⌘02 / BUILD

Build your own trading rules

Drag blocks to make a strategy, or tell AI what you want. Then let your saved rules handle trades within the limits you set.

Explore strategies ↗
◷03 / REVIEW

Try your strategy on past prices

Use past market prices to try a strategy you made or one drafted with AI. Trades are simulated.

Explore backtesting ↗
↗04 / EXECUTE

Charts and trades in one place

View charts, place spot or futures trades, and keep track of open trades in one workspace.

See the terminal ↗
✳05 / ANALYZE

AI chart help and trading assistant

Ask AI to explain a chart, possible entry and stop-loss, risks, and reasoning. Set a 24/7 trading helper to watch selected markets within your risk limits.

Explore AI tools ↗
⌁06 / CONNECT

Discord alerts and trading chat

Get Discord alerts and join in-app discussions about daily market ideas and trading risks.

Explore alerts & chat ↗

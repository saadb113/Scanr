Let your saved rules
handle the routine.

When turned on, your strategy can watch the coins you choose and follow the rules you saved, so you do not have to place every trade yourself. Automated trading can still lose money. Check your rules and limits before turning it on.

  • Choose which coins your strategy watches
  • Set trade and loss limits
  • Pause trading and review what happened

06 / REVIEW

Try a strategy on
old price charts.

See how a strategy might have worked on past market prices before you use real money. Test a strategy you made, one drafted with AI, or another supported Scanr strategy. All trades in backtesting are simulated.

  • Step through old candles yourself or let a strategy respond
  • See where trades might have opened and closed
  • Review wins, losses, and account drops
  • Past results cannot tell you what will happen next

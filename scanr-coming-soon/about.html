THE PERSON BEHIND THE PRODUCT

Built with curiosity.
Designed for clarity.

Scanr is an independent project exploring how thoughtful software can make crypto market research and trading workflows easier to navigate.

WHY SCANR

Make the process
easier to see.

Crypto markets move quickly, and the tools around them can be fragmented. Scanr is my attempt to bring research, planning, strategy experiments, and review into a more coherent workspace.

This is an early-stage build. The product and its direction will keep changing as I learn from the work and from people who follow along.

ClarityMake context easier to read.
DisciplineMake risk visible in the workflow.
TransparencyShare progress honestly, without made-up metrics.
